Pricing figures older than 30 days are not `confirmed` — vendors reprice without notice; re-fetch before any build.

A vendor cut its top subscription tier's price at a product event, and third-party trackers still showed the old figure weeks later. A separate vendor revised its usage-credit tiers mid-project, after the factsheet was compiled but before the artifact shipped. Why it mattered: a factsheet carried forward between projects goes stale silently, and stale pricing inside a commercialization artifact is exactly the error that artifact exists to catch in others. The rule is now a freshness threshold — 30 days for pricing, 90 for everything else — enforced at fact-audit time rather than remembered.
